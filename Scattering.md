Scattering Amplitudes in Gauge Theory and Gravity (https://www.google.com/search?kgmid=%2Fg%2F1q6s3m8vt&q=how+to+begin+learning+surfaceology+scattering+amplitudes+in+gauge+theory+and+gravity) by Henriette Elvang and Yu-tin Huang is the standard graduate textbook for modern on-shell amplitude methods. You can access the free preprint version directly on arXiv (1308.1697) or find the published edition via Cambridge University Press. [1] (https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.1697), [2] (https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/scattering-amplitudes-in-gauge-theory-and-gravity/34C045B8331E6FF229E2496F6D8321C5), [3] (https://pdfs.semanticscholar.org/1db5/3410b397bf5b793f2d1075132534e386129e.pdf)
Because the book covers a wide range of topics—including supersymmetry and supergravity—you do not need to read it cover-to-cover to prepare for surfaceology. Focus specifically on these chapters: [1] (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015sagt.book.....E/abstract)

1. Must-Read Chapters for Surfaceology

• Chapter 1 & 2 (Introduction & Spinor Helicity Formalism). Read Chapter 1 for motivation and basic kinematics. In Chapter 2, focus specifically on color ordering (color decomposition) and how matrix-valued fields strip away group theory factors to leave planar, cyclically ordered amplitudes. [1] (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015sagt.book.....E/abstract)
• Chapter 3 (On-Shell Recursion Relations at Tree-Level). Learn how amplitudes are constructed from their pole structures and factorization properties rather than Lagrangians, which mirrors how polytope boundaries work. [1] (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015sagt.book.....E/abstract)
• Chapter 6 & 8 (Loop Amplitudes & On-Shell Diagrams). Focus on the concept of the loop integrand (rational functions prior to integration) and how cuts isolate singularities. [1] (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015sagt.book.....E/abstract)
• Chapter 10 (Polytopes). This is the most direct bridge to surfaceology. It introduces the associahedron and shows how scattering amplitudes arise as canonical forms (volumes of dual polytopes). [1] (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015sagt.book.....E/abstract)

2. Chapters You Can Safely Skip (At First)

• Chapters 4, 5, 11, & 12. These focus heavily on \(\mathcal{N}=4\) Super Yang-Mills, superspace, and supergravity. Surfaceology originally bypassed supersymmetry to describe non-supersymmetric theories (like Tr(φ³), pions, and pure Yang-Mills), so you can skip these without losing the thread. [1] (https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/abs/2015sagt.book.....E/abstract)
• Chapter 9 (Grassmannians). While foundational for the amplituhedron, surfaceology replaces Grassmannian geometry with curves on surfaces and cluster algebras, making this optional background rather than a strict prerequisite.
